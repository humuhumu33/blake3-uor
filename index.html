Verdict

How it runs

  1. Your browser fetches the Prism image f20bd6c0… and checks every part against its SHA-256.
  2. The image's WebAssembly engine runs the LexLean programs hash, keyedHash and deriveKey. No line of the hashing is hand-written.
  3. A separate BLAKE3 library computes the same thing, and the page compares the two.

Check it against the official vectors

Nine of the official BLAKE3 test vectors: inputs of 0, 64 and 65 bytes in all three modes.

Slow on purpose: the model records every step of BLAKE3 as a trace, about a second per 64-byte block here, so inputs stop at 1 KiB. The same model, exported as certified Rust, matches all 35 official vectors and the blake3 crate in every mode (tests B3-01 to B3-04).
